## (c) Verdict
The claim of purchase-price parity is not supported by anything I could verify. The leads suggest the cheapest gas 125cc models can be bought for under NT$50,000 after promotions, while the Gogoro models seen start around NT$75,000–90,000 before subsidy. Central plus local subsidies (roughly NT$7,000–13,000 central, and NT$3,000–36,000+ local depending on city and scrappage) could close the gap for scrappers in generous cities (Taoyuan, New Taipei, Tainan), but that is unconfirmed arithmetic, not a finding. Until the sources are checked, the site should say "subsidies can narrow or close the purchase gap in some cities for buyers who scrap an old scooter" and not "price parity". It should not state parity for all buyers.
